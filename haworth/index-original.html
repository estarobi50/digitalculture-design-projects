About us: Tal Lebel, DMD

New to our practice in 2015, Dr. Lebel claims he’s never worked a day in his life. That’s because he enjoys helping patients achieve their healthiest smiles, and that just doesn’t feel like work.

Dr. Lebel completed his undergraduate studies at Brooklyn College and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey. He then completed his dental residency at the prestigious VA Medical Center in San Diego, CA.

Dedicated to lifelong learning, Dr. Lebel has additional training in Invisalign, dental implants, and advanced restoratives. To stay current and build a support network with his peers, he maintains memberships in various dental organizations.
