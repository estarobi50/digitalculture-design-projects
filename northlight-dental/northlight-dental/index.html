Chapter 023D imaging

We scan first, then plan.

A 3D scan shows bone, roots and nerve paths that flat X-rays can't. We review it with you on screen, so you see what we see before we recommend anything.

  • Bone height and width measured in millimetres before any implant is planned.
  • Nerve paths and sinus floors marked on the scan.
  • A copy of your scan on request, for you or a specialist.

We take a scan only when it will change the plan, and use the smallest field that covers the area.

Chapter 03Shade matching
Daylight, 5500 K

Matched under the right light.

Teeth are translucent, and they change colour with the light around them. We take shade under daylight-balanced light, then check the result again under warm light.

On-screen swatches are approximate. A monitor can't reproduce a ceramic shade tab.

Book a children's visit

Before you book.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with many major plans and check your benefits before your visit. Send your plan details when you book and we will confirm what is covered.

How long is a first visit?

Plan on about an hour. That covers your history, an exam, X-rays or a 3D scan if you need one, and a conversation about what we found.

Is a 3D scan safe?

A dental 3D scan uses X-rays, like standard dental X-rays. We take one only when it will change the plan, and we keep the scanned area as small as we can.

What if something hurts and I'm not a patient yet?

Call us. We hold time open each day for urgent problems such as a broken tooth or swelling.

Do you see young children?

Yes, from their first tooth. A first visit is short, and a parent stays with the child throughout.
